name: 120 fps
description: Races at 120 frames a second (on a 120 Hz screen) instead of 30. Each frame takes one 120 Hz vblank, and the race steps 1/120 s a frame; a frame the device doesn't finish in time shows late, and the race slows down with it. Use it instead of the 60 fps mod, not with it.
author: hakuX
refresh: 120

[default.xbe]
# The vblanks a race frame takes: the PresentInterval setting (0x291A04),
# as the 60 fps mod. The race steps vblanks / refresh_rate seconds a frame
# (0x4860F1, 0x48614B; 0xD9073, 0xEFE91...): with refresh at 120 Hz and
# interval 1, that is 1/120 s, the same formula the 60 fps mod gives at 60 Hz.
0x00291A04: 02 -> 01
